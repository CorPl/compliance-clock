What this service is — and is not

Compliance Check is an information service. It reports compliance deadlines and rule changes for small businesses as published by official government and EU sources, with links to those sources and the date each entry was verified.

Nothing on this website or in its data feed is legal, tax, accounting, financial or other professional advice, and none of it is a substitute for advice from a qualified professional — a solicitor, accountant or tax adviser — who knows your specific circumstances. Using this site does not create any professional or client relationship.

Accuracy — our effort and its limits

  • We verify every entry against official sources and print the source link and verification date on the entry itself.
  • Laws and deadlines change, official pages are sometimes corrected, and despite our care errors are possible. Information is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind — including accuracy, completeness or timeliness.
  • Status labels (confirmed / announced / proposed / unverified) reflect our good-faith assessment on the verification date shown, not a guarantee.
  • Always check the linked official source before acting or making decisions that depend on a date or rule shown here.

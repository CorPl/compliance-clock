What Compliance Check is

A continuously verified record of the deadlines and rule changes that hit small businesses across the UK and Europe — published for people and machines.

Small-business compliance truth is scattered across dozens of official websites in a dozen languages: HMRC and Companies House in the UK, the tax authorities of France, Germany, Poland, Belgium and Romania, and the EU institutions. Compliance Check watches those official sources, verifies every change, and publishes one clean, dated record — free to read, free to build on.

It is built as much for software and AI agents as for people. As tools increasingly file, invoice and keep books on a business's behalf, they need a source of truth that is provably current and honest about its own freshness. That is the entire design brief of this site.

How every entry earns its place

Official sources only

Every entry cites government or EU pages — never news articles, never consultancy blogs. The source link and the date we verified it are printed on the entry itself.

Honest status labels

Each entry is marked confirmed (in force or in law), announced (stated by government, not yet law), proposed, or unverified — we publish uncertainty rather than hide it.

Append-only record

Published entries are never silently edited. Corrections are published as new entries that reference what they supersede — so the record of what we said, and when, is permanent.

Freshness in the open

Automated watchers check every official source daily. When each source was last successfully checked — including any failures — is public on the source freshness page.

Fail-closed publishing

Nothing reaches this site without passing validation, and detected changes are verified by a human editor before publication. A failure can delay an update; it can never corrupt one.

Free and reusable

The data is licensed CC BY 4.0: use it in your product, app or agent with attribution to Compliance Check.

For developers and AI agents

The whole dataset is served as one static JSON document — https://compliancecheck.uk/feed.json — with per-jurisdiction freshness metadata, entry statuses and official source URLs. There is no API key, no rate ceremony and nothing dynamic to fail: it is a flat file behind a CDN, rebuilt after every verified update. A machine-oriented site summary lives at /llms.txt.

Who runs it

Compliance Check is an independent, UK-based information service, launched in August 2026. It reports what official sources say — it does not interpret the law and it is not legal, tax or professional advice. Contact: a2a@compliancecheck.uk.

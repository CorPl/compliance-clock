2026-08-07 United Kingdom Tax filingconfirmed

First Making Tax Digital quarterly update due

The first quarterly update under Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (covering 6 April to 5 July 2026) must reach HMRC by 7 August 2026.

Applies to: UK sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000

Subsequent updates due 7 November 2026, 7 February 2027 and 7 May 2027.

Source: www.gov.uk · verified 2026-08-03

2026-09-01 France E-invoicingconfirmed

All French companies must be able to receive e-invoices

From 1 September 2026 every company registered in France must be able to receive electronic invoices (facturation électronique) via the official platform network.

Applies to: All VAT-registered businesses in France, regardless of size

Issuing obligation phases in later; large and mid-sized companies must also issue from this date.

Source: www.impots.gouv.fr · verified 2026-08-03
